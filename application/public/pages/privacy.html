Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  CSC 648-848 Spring 2026  ·  Team 1

📋

What Data We Collect

Account Information Name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored as a secure bcrypt hash — never in plaintext.
Academic Data Courses, grades, and GPA entered by the user for progress tracking.
Goals and Milestones Personal goals, milestones, and project progress created within the platform.
Profile Photo An optional profile image uploaded by the user.
Usage Data Basic interaction data used to improve the platform experience.
🎯

Why We Collect It

To personalize your dashboard and surface resources relevant to your academic profile.
To track your progress over time and generate reports on your goals and milestones.
👁️

Who Can See Your Data

You You always have full access to your own data.
Administrators Platform admins may access data for moderation and support purposes only.
No Third Parties We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties — ever.
🔒

How We Protect Your Data

All passwords are hashed using bcrypt before being stored — plaintext passwords never exist in the database.
JWT authentication is enforced on all protected routes, with short-lived access tokens and revocable refresh tokens.
Admin access to other users' data is role-restricted and cannot be accessed through standard user accounts.
⚖️

Your Rights

You can view and edit your own profile and personal information at any time.
You can delete your account and all associated data upon request.
You can request a password reset at any time using your registered email or security questions.

Have questions or concerns about your privacy? Contact our team directly.
