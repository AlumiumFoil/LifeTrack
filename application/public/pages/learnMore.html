Explore What LifeTrack Offers

LifeTrack is built to help students manage school, productivity, wellness, and career preparation in one place. Below is a closer look at the categories and resources available through our platform.

Academic

Academic resources are designed to help students stay organized in class, prepare for exams, and build better study habits throughout the semester.

Study Guide

A step by step workflow for building study guides before exams.

Note Taking Template

A clean note taking template for lectures and readings.

Flashcard Template

A simple flashcard template for key terms and concepts.

Productivity

Productivity resources help students plan projects, manage time, and reduce distractions so they can stay focused on what matters most.

Project Planning Template

A template that breaks a project into milestones and tasks.

Weekly Planning Module

A module that helps plan the week and prioritize tasks.

Distraction Control Tips

Tips to reduce distractions while working or studying.

Wellness

Wellness resources support students in managing stress, maintaining healthier routines, and taking time to check in with themselves during busy weeks.

Breathing Reset Guide

A quick guide for breathing exercises during stress.

Sleep Routine Workflow

A simple sleep routine workflow for busy weeks.

Career

Career resources help students start preparing for internships, jobs, and professional growth with practical tools they can begin using right away.

Resume Starter Template

A clean resume template with sections and tips.

Portfolio Checklist

A checklist to build a simple portfolio site.

Interview Prep Workflow

A workflow for preparing for interviews.
