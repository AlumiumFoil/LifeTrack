Suggested Sleep Routine
  • Choose a time you can realistically hit every night
  • Aim for 7–9 hours depending on when you wake up
  • Weekends within 1 hour of your weekday bedtime helps too
  • Blue light delays your body's melatonin production
  • Put your phone on Do Not Disturb and leave it across the room
  • Replace scrolling with reading, journaling, or stretching
  • Lay out what you need the night before
  • Reduces morning stress and decision fatigue
  • Takes 2–3 minutes and makes mornings much smoother
  • Ideal sleep temperature is around 65–68 °F (18–20 °C)
  • Use blackout curtains or a sleep mask if needed
  • White noise or earplugs help block disruptive sounds
  • Brain-dump the one task that keeps nagging at you
  • Writing it down lets your mind let go of it
  • Keeps your sleep from being disrupted by mental to-do lists
