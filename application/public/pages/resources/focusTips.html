  • Breathe in for 4 counts
  • Hold for 4 counts
  • Breathe out for 6 counts
  • Repeat 4 times
  • Try it before studying or after something stressful
  • Step away from your screen for 5 minutes
  • Stretch, get water, or look out a window
  • Don't check your phone during the break
  • When you sit back down, write one thing to start with
  • Put your phone across the room or face down
  • Close any tabs you don't need right now
  • Use a site blocker like Cold Turkey if needed
  • Let people around you know you need quiet time
  • Pick the smallest thing you can do right now
  • Not "finish the project", just "open the file"
  • Not "study for the exam", just "read the first page"
  • Getting started is usually the hardest part
  • Work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break
  • After 4 rounds, take a longer break (15 to 20 minutes)
  • Don't multitask during sprints
  • Use the timer on the Studying page to keep track
  • You've re-read the same paragraph 3 times
  • You can't remember what you just read
  • You feel tense or your eyes are strained
  • Take a real break, not a "scroll your phone" break
