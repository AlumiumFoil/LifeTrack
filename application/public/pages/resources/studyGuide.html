Study Guide Workflow

A step-by-step workflow for building study guides before exams. Work through each step and check it off as you go.

6
Total Steps
0
Done
6
Remaining
  • Collect all lecture notes, slides, and readings
  • Find past exams or practice questions if available
  • Check the syllabus for topics and learning objectives
  • Organize everything by topic or week
  • List the main topics that will be on the exam
  • Break each topic into subtopics and key concepts
  • Mark which areas you feel least confident about
  • Use this outline as your guide for what to cover
  • Write a 2–3 sentence summary for each major topic
  • Use your own words — don't copy from slides
  • Include definitions, formulas, or key terms
  • If you can't summarize it, you may need to review it more
  • Cover your notes and try to recall key points from memory
  • Quiz yourself using questions from your outline
  • Use flashcards for definitions and formulas
  • Focus extra time on topics you struggle to recall
  • Work through past exam questions under timed conditions
  • Don't look at the answers until you've tried each question
  • Review wrong answers and understand why you missed them
  • Repeat questions you got wrong a second time later
  • Do a light review of your full outline — don't cram new material
  • Re-read your summaries for the trickiest topics
  • Get at least 7–8 hours of sleep the night before
  • Eat a good meal and arrive to the exam early
