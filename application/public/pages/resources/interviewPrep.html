Interview Prep Workflow

A step-by-step way to get ready for an interview. Work through each one and check it off when you're done.

6
Total Steps
0
Done
6
Remaining
  • Check their website, mission, and any recent news
  • Look up your interviewer on LinkedIn if you can
  • Know 1 or 2 things that make you want to work there
  • Read every line out loud before you go in
  • Be ready to explain any project or job listed
  • Know which 2 or 3 experiences you'll talk about most
  • Pick 3 to 5 real stories from school or work
  • Use STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result
  • Cover: a challenge solved, something learned fast, a team experience
  • Say your answers out loud, not just in your head
  • Common ones: about yourself, why this company, a challenge you handled
  • Practice with a friend or record yourself on your phone
  • Prepare 2 or 3 questions in advance
  • Good topics: team culture, day-to-day work, current projects
  • Not asking anything makes you look disinterested
  • Send a thank-you email within 24 hours
  • Mention one specific thing from the conversation
  • Keep it short, 3 to 5 sentences is enough
