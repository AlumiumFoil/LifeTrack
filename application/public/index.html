LifeTrack

Your all-in-one student life management platform.

LifeTrack is designed for students who want one place to manage daily responsibilities, track academic progress, stay on top of personal wellness, and organize goals that matter. Our platform helps students balance school and life with tools built specifically around the challenges of student life.

Productivity

📘

Academic

💙

Wellness

Build healthy habits, reflect on your well-being, and support a more balanced student routine.

About Our Team

Team 01 is a group of engineers from SFSU who wanted to make life management easier for students by creating a platform that brings together organization, academic support, and wellness tools in one place.
